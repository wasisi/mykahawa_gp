Coffee dealers and other licensed businesses must hold a licence from the Coffee Board of Kenya (formerly the Agriculture and Food Authority) or, for some licences, from the county government. See [coffee licences in Kenya](/projects/kenya-coffee-licensing.html). Browse by licence type in the side panel, or see who [buys the most at the auction](/directory/coffee-buyers.html).
