About 60% of Kenya's coffee comes from smallholder farmers, most of them members of a cooperative society. The society processes members' cherry at its factories, markets the coffee through a marketing agent or as a grower marketer, and pays members from the proceeds.

See the societies' [factories](/directory/factories.html), or find any grower on the [map](/growers.html).
