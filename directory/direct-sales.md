In a [direct sale](/projects/kenya-coffee-laws.html), a registered grower, cooperative, estate or grower miller sells its own clean coffee to an overseas buyer or a local roaster without going through the auction. A sample goes to the exchange for quality and price discovery, and the price must compare favourably with exchange prices. Facilitators find buyers and help growers arrange these sales. A [buyer's licence](/directory/coffee-exporters.html) does not cover direct sales.

This list comes from **proposed grants of licence** published in the Kenya Gazette since August 2025. Each notice gives the public 14 days to object, and a licence is only issued once the applicant meets the requirements of the coffee regulations. A listing here does not confirm that a licence was issued. Check with the [Coffee Board of Kenya](/projects/kenya-coffee-licensing.html) before you sign a contract.
