Help fill the gaps

Do you have old Nairobi Coffee Exchange results?

After every auction the NCE sends the sale results to brokers, buyers and cooperatives. MyKahawa is missing some of them. With them, every cooperative, factory and estate gets a complete, free price history on its page, and farmers can see how their coffee has done over the years.

What to send: the Transaction Listing of a sale, as PDF or Excel (named like “Sale 14 Provisional Transaction Listing of Tuesday, January 20, 2026”). Forwarding the NCE email with its attachments is fine. Market totals and grade summaries are not needed.

Email the files to info@mykahawa.org

We use only the sale results, which the NCE publishes. Please remove anything personal before forwarding an email. Thank you: asante sana!
