About coffee growing in Kenya

Kenya’s coffee is grown in the highlands between 1,400 and 2,000 metres above sea level. The main growing areas are around Mount Kenya, the Aberdares, Nakuru, Machakos, Kisii and Mount Elgon. Most coffee is grown on small farms organised into cooperative societies, each running one or more factories (wet mills), and on plantations or estates. Under the Coffee Act, the line between small and large farms depends on the size and ownership of pulping stations. Around 60% of Kenya’s coffee comes from smallholder farmers.
