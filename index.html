Frequently asked questions

Where does MyKahawa get its coffee data?

The Nairobi Coffee Exchange (NCE) runs Kenya’s central coffee auction, usually on Tuesdays at Wakulima House, Haile Selassie Avenue, Nairobi. The NCE publishes results after each sale. MyKahawa compiled the results from {{ m.first_sale | date: "%B %Y" }} to {{ m.last_sale | date: "%B %Y" }}; all records are free to download.

Are you affiliated with the Nairobi Coffee Exchange?

No. MyKahawa is independent.

Can I buy coffee from MyKahawa?

No. We provide data and information. We are not licensed to trade coffee. To buy Kenyan coffee, contact a licensed exporter or a marketing agent.

Why is my cooperative, estate or company not listed?

The directory is based on Nairobi Coffee Exchange transactions from 2015 to 2020 and the 2012 Kenya Coffee Directory of the Kenya Coffee Traders Association. To add, correct or remove a listing, email wasisi.coffee@gmail.com. Listings are free.

Where can I learn more about the Kenyan coffee trade?
  • Nairobi Coffee Exchange: runs the central coffee auction
  • Agriculture and Food Authority (AFA): regulation and coffee dealer licences, through its Coffee Directorate
  • Kenya Agricultural and Livestock Research Organization (KALRO): home of the Coffee Research Institute
  • Kenya Coffee Producers Association
  • Kenya Coffee Traders Association
  • African Fine Coffees Association: specialty coffee news and events in Africa
  • Kenya Co-operative Coffee Exporters (KCCE)
  • TechnoServe Kenya: smallholder coffee programmes
  • FarmDrive: credit for smallholder farmers
