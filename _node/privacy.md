<p>This policy explains what personal data MyKahawa collects, why, who else receives it, how long we keep it, and your rights under Kenya's <strong>Data Protection Act, 2019</strong>. "Personal data" means any information about an identified or identifiable person, such as a name, phone number, email address or the location of a privately owned farm.</p>

<p><strong>Who we are.</strong> MyKahawa publishes information about Kenya's coffee industry. For any question or request about your personal data, email <a href="mailto:wasisi.coffee@gmail.com">wasisi.coffee@gmail.com</a>.</p>

<p>Giving us personal data is voluntary. If you leave out a contact email on a form, we can't reply to you, and if you leave out a grower's details we may not be able to create its profile.</p>

<h2>Information from public sources</h2>

<p>The directory and grower pages also use information from public records: Nairobi Coffee Exchange auction results, Kenya Gazette notices, licence registers, company websites and OpenStreetMap. These are mostly about businesses, cooperatives and estates, but can include the names of people. We label information we have not verified (for example, licences that are only <em>proposed</em> in the Gazette). If something about you is wrong or you want it removed, contact us (see "Your rights" below).</p>

<h2>What we publish</h2>

<p>Grower profiles are public. We publish a contact person's name, phone number or email address <strong>only if you have agreed to it</strong> on the listing form or in writing. We do not publish the private home address of any person, and we show the location of a privately owned farm only with the owner's agreement.</p>

<h2>Who else receives your data</h2>

<p>We do not sell personal data. These services process data for us. Some of them store it outside Kenya, mainly in the United States and the European Union, under their own security and data protection terms:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Google</strong> (Google Forms and Google Sheets) stores what you submit on our forms, and our email is hosted on Gmail.</li>
  <li><strong>Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics</strong> measure visits to the website.</li>
  <li><strong>GitHub Pages</strong> hosts the website and keeps server logs, including IP addresses.</li>
  <li><strong>Google Fonts</strong> and <strong>OpenStreetMap</strong> map tiles are loaded from their servers, so they see your IP address.</li>
  <li><strong>Ko-fi</strong> handles donations if you choose to support us. Its own privacy policy applies.</li>
</ul>
<p>By submitting a form, you agree that your data may be stored by these services outside Kenya. We may also disclose personal data where the law requires it.</p>

<h2>How long we keep it</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Subscribers:</strong> until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details.</li>
  <li><strong>Listing submissions:</strong> for as long as the profile is published. If we don't publish a profile, we delete the submission within 12 months.</li>
  <li><strong>Emails:</strong> as long as needed to deal with your request.</li>
  <li><strong>Website analytics:</strong> no longer than 14 months.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How we protect it</h2>
<p>Form responses are kept in a private Google account protected by two-step verification. Only MyKahawa can see them. The website is served over HTTPS, and the raw research data is kept in a private repository, not on the public website. If a breach puts your data at real risk, we will tell you and the Data Commissioner as the law requires.</p>

<h2>Your rights</h2>
<p>Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>be told how your personal data is used (this policy);</li>
  <li>see the personal data we hold about you;</li>
  <li>object to our use of your personal data;</li>
  <li>have false, misleading, outdated or incomplete data corrected;</li>
  <li>have data deleted that is false or misleading, or that we no longer need;</li>
  <li>withdraw your consent at any time, for example by unsubscribing or asking us to remove a profile or contact details;</li>
  <li>receive the data you gave us in a common electronic format.</li>
</ul>
<p>To use any of these rights, email <a href="mailto:wasisi.coffee@gmail.com">wasisi.coffee@gmail.com</a>. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the <a href="https://www.odpc.go.ke/" rel="noopener">Office of the Data Protection Commissioner</a>.</p>

<h2>Cookies</h2>
<p>Our analytics (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics) use cookies to count visits. They are not used for advertising. You can block cookies in your browser settings, or install the <a href="https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout" rel="noopener">Google Analytics opt-out add-on</a>. The rest of the site works without cookies.</p>

<h2>Changes to this policy</h2>
<p>We will post any changes on this page. This policy was last updated on 11 November 2026.</p>
